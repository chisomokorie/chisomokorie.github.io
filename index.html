How to play

How I work

The same four moves sit behind every level on this page, whether the work is an admissions process, a city service queue or a product.

Move 1

Find where work stalls

Trace the process end to end and locate the handoff or rule that slows it. Example: course approvals stalled across 5 departments.

Move 2

Ask what people really need

Gather requirements from the people affected and separate the stated request from the real goal. Example: the AFMUA founder wanted lasting reach.

Move 3

Build the fix

Turn the rule or requirement into a process, workflow or tool people can see. Example: the timetable dashboard and Kuali workflows.

Move 4

Make it stick

Document it, train people and start small so the change is adopted. Example: training material that cut onboarding time by 30%.

Career Buddy

An AI-enabled career empowerment app I am designing and building with Lovable and Claude Code · Working name · Beta launching in the coming months

"Where you are is only part of the story, not the end of the book."
GoalMany people told me they did not know where to start in the job market. Some felt hopeless because they thought they were behind. Career Buddy starts from wherever they are.
ResultThe prototype is built, and the beta launches in the coming months. Real user data will guide the next improvements.
ActionI designed the architecture first, because the idea was complex. I split the journey into chapters, so moving forward feels visible without feeling like pressure.

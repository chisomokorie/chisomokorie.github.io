How to play

How I work

The same four moves sit behind every level on this page, whether the work is an admissions process, a city service queue or a product.

Move 1

Find where work stalls

Trace the process end to end and locate the handoff or rule that slows it. Example: course approvals stalled across 5 departments.

Move 2

Ask what people really need

Gather requirements from the people affected and separate the stated request from the real goal. Example: the AFMUA founder wanted lasting reach.

Move 3

Build the fix

Turn the rule or requirement into a process, workflow or tool people can see. Example: the timetable dashboard and Kuali workflows.

Move 4

Make it stick

Document it, train people and start small so the change is adopted. Example: training material that cut onboarding time by 30%.

The work-study task bank

Western University, Electrical & Computer Engineering · built with Claude, prompts engineered with ChatGPT

GoalAs the default supervisor for our work-study students, I planned their tasks for each shift. Colleagues sometimes had work for them too, but it often came up as students arrived, after the plan was set. That meant last-minute changes and work pushed back. The fix was to make everyone's needs visible before the shift, not during it.
ResultEvery supervisor's work now sits in one place before the shift. Approved tasks are ready in the bank, and the dashboard flags overdue work and students who need more.
Bonus
ActionInstead of adding more reminders, I designed one shared system: a supervisor check-in where colleagues post work in advance, an ongoing task bank of approved work, a daily task log with due dates and status, and a dashboard and weekly summary. I used ChatGPT to engineer the prompts and Claude to build it in Excel.
